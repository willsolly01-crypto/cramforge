## Instagram / Facebook Ads — Uni Students

### Campaign: "From Notes to Exam" (awareness)

**Headline A**
> Your notes are sitting there. Your exam isn't.

**Headline B**
> Infinite practice questions. From your own notes.

**Headline C**
> The AI that reads your notes and quizzes you on them.

**Primary text (125 chars):**
> Upload your lecture notes. Get unlimited exam questions with worked solutions — generated from your content, not a generic question bank.

**Body copy (long — for Facebook feed):**
> You've got the notes. You've been to the lectures. But reading your notes for the third time isn't studying — it's procrastinating with good intentions.
>
> CramForge reads your lecture slides, PDFs, or typed notes, and generates unlimited exam-style questions on your exact content. You write an answer. The AI marks it — with a score, examiner's feedback, and a model answer.
>
> You find out exactly what you actually understand, and exactly what to fix.
>
> Free to start. Takes 60 seconds to get your first question.

**CTA button:** "Try CramForge Free"
**Landing URL:** cramforge.app

### Campaign: "Quick Study" (engagement / retargeting)

**Headline A**
> 5 questions from your notes. In 2 minutes. On your phone.

**Headline B**
> Study between classes. In the lunch queue. At the gym.

**Primary text:**
> CramForge's Quick Study mode gives you 5 MCQ questions from your notes — on your phone, whenever you have 2 minutes. Build a streak. Earn XP. Unlock combo multipliers. It's genuinely addictive.

## Mannix College / Residential College Posters

### Copy for poster headline (A3, landscape — see poster-campus.html)
> Your notes. Infinite questions.

### Alternative headlines to test:
- "Studied for 3 hours. Still don't know what you'd score? CramForge fixes that."
- "Your notes → Unlimited exam questions → Actual marks feedback. Try it free."
- "The AI that reads your lecture slides and quizzes you on them."

### Where to put them:
- Common rooms / study rooms
- Dining hall noticeboard
- Near library printers
- Tutorial rooms (ask tutors)
- Bathrooms in residences (highest dwell time)
- Lifts

### QR code
Generate a QR code pointing to `cramforge.app/?ref=mannix` (or `?ref=campus`) — this lets you track how many signups come from physical posters. Create it free at qr.io or qrcode-monkey.com, download as SVG, replace the placeholder in the HTML files.
