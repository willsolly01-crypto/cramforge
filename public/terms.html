What CramForge is

CramForge generates practice questions from study materials you upload, marks your attempts, and tracks your progress. It's a revision and practice tool. Questions and marking are AI-generated: they're designed to be helpful, but they can contain errors, and they are not official exam content. Always check important answers against your course materials.

Fair and honest use

By using CramForge you agree to:

  • Only upload materials you're allowed to use (your own notes, or materials your school or teacher permits you to have).
  • Use the tool for revision and practice — never to complete assessments, exams, or work that must be your own. Follow your school's academic integrity rules.
  • Not attempt to abuse, resell, or bulk-extract the service, or share one account between many people.

We may suspend accounts that break these rules.

Free tier and Pro subscription

The free plan includes daily limits on question generation, marking, and material uploads. CramForge Pro removes those limits for a monthly subscription billed through Stripe. You can cancel any time from the Account page — your Pro access continues until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
